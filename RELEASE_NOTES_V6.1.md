# Hariyo Mart Nepal v6.1.0

## Advanced marketplace and operations release

- Rebuilt marketplace discovery around uniform square product cards, delivery distance, live stock,
  district, province, organic, category, radius, price and rating controls.
- Added one persistent delivery-location profile shared by the header, homepage location engine and
  marketplace ranking.
- Expanded the footer with nationwide operating coverage, buyer and seller resources and a working
  newsletter subscription flow.
- Replaced repeated placeholder blog copy with eight useful Hariyo Journal stories, featured
  editorial design, article navigation, related products and related stories.
- Added D1 tables for editorial content, newsletter subscribers, service areas, promotions, reviews,
  support tickets, inventory events, notifications and centralized platform settings.
- Added protected admin APIs and live workspaces for content publishing, service zones, promotions,
  support, review moderation, settings and marketplace analytics.
- Added a working public support-ticket flow and review endpoints, plus auditable seller inventory
  adjustments.
- Upgraded the one-command Cloudflare deployer to apply all D1 migrations before publishing and to
  verify live service-area and product data.

Cash on delivery remains the only enabled payment method. eSewa, Khalti and Fonepay must remain
disabled until merchant onboarding, signed callback verification, reconciliation, refund handling
and certification are complete.
